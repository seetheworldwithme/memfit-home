# About Memfit AI

**Memfit AI** is the open-source cybersecurity AI Agent orchestration framework of the [Yaklang](https://yaklang.com) ecosystem. We are committed to giving AI visible agency in security automation and code auditing scenarios through the recursive dual-engine (ReAct + Plan) architecture.

## Relationship with Yaklang

Memfit AI is an **official Yaklang project**, designed and maintained by the Yaklang team. It works closely with Yaklang's core security capabilities and represents the Yaklang ecosystem's official move into AI agents.

## Core Philosophy

- **Recursive dual-engine**: the ReAct engine handles real-time reasoning and action, while the Plan engine handles task decomposition and planning, collaborating recursively.
- **Visible agency**: an emphasis on observable execution, spin detection, and reliability guarantees rather than black-box output.
- **Open source**: released under Apache-2.0, with community participation welcome.
